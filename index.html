Assumptions

From those assumptions

Annual hours saved = clients × hours per close × saved % × 12. Capacity freed = those hours × billing rate. Annual cost = clients × monthly price × 12. Net benefit = capacity freed − annual cost. Net ROI = net benefit ÷ annual cost. Break-even price is the monthly per-client amount that makes net benefit zero. Break-even hours saved is the percentage that covers the assumed cost.
